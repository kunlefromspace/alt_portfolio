About

I Explored the Earth. Now I Build Digital Worlds.

A geophysics graduate who moved into software and never really stopped thinking in signals.

My background is in subsurface data: seismic traces, well logs, geological cross-sections — patterns buried in noise. That training carried straight over into software and AI: same instinct for finding structure in messy inputs, now applied to APIs, datasets, and automation pipelines instead of rock layers.

Today I work across full-stack development and AI automation, building web apps end-to-end and designing AI-driven workflows that take manual, repetitive work off people's plates. I'm also actively deepening my machine learning practice, with a specific interest in applying it back to subsurface characterization.
